## Omarchy mapping

Omarchy's semantic palette needs a neutral ramp plus terminal/status colors. The mapping preserves the Handbook's identity while adding a few restrained cool tones so ANSI blue/cyan/magenta remain distinguishable in terminals and diagnostics.

- **Accent:** Ember (`#e97835`)
- **Selection:** Burnt umber (`#402312`)
- **Background:** Handbook page background (`#0e0c0b`)
- **Lighter background:** Handbook surface (`#1e1912`)
- **Foreground:** Handbook body copy (`#dfd0bb`)
- **Bright foreground:** Handbook main text (`#f2e6d4`)
- **Green:** Handbook tip color (`#87b978`)
- **Yellow:** Handbook warning color (`#f1c76a`)
